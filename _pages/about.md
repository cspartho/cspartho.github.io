I am a **Machine Learning Engineer and aspiring researcher** with over **three years of professional experience** in **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, **Recommendation Systems**, and **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. My academic and industry work focuses on designing intelligent systems that are both **theoretically grounded** and **practically deployable**.

In industry, I have worked on building **end-to-end machine learning pipelines**, including ASR-based conversational agents, personalized recommender systems, OCR pipelines, and LLM-driven information extraction and tagging systems. These experiences have strengthened my understanding of data-centric AI, model evaluation, and real-world constraints in deploying learning systems.
